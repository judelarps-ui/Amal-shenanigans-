📖 Game Guide & All Character Abilities

🔮 Nightmare Duo Fusion

When you have both Miss Rita and Miss Joumana active on your board, tap the 🔮 Fuse Duo button to sacrifice both and summon the supreme Nightmare Duo Fusion entity (2000 HP, 900 ATK, 1-turn Cooldown)! Ability: Heals +500 HP and permanently boosts ATK by +300!

🎓 Complete Fighter Roster & Abilities

  • 👑 Miss Ghizel (S-Tier): Principal Decree — Heals herself for 25% Max HP and triples (3x) her ATK stat for 1 turn. (Cooldown: 3 Turns)
  • 📐 Mr. Brahim (S-Tier): Pi Recitation — Recite digits of Pi after "3." Each correct digit adds a +20% multiplier to current ATK! (Cooldown: 2 Turns)
  • 🛑 Miss Rawa (A-Tier): Shut Up! — Silences and freezes all enemy cards and turn actions for 2 full turns. (Cooldown: 2 Turns)
  • 📝 Miss Lila (A-Tier): Written Quiz — Steals 50% of the active enemy fighter's current HP and restores it to herself. (Cooldown: 2 Turns)
  • ⚠️ Miss Joumana (B-Tier): Warning Slip — Sacrifices 30% of her current HP to empower herself with a +70% stat boost. Component for Nightmare Duo Fusion. (Cooldown: 3 Turns)
  • 🌿 Miss Lilan (B-Tier): Cloud Plants — Instantly buffs her strike power with +400 bonus attack damage. (Cooldown: 2 Turns)
  • 🐸 Mr. Kameel (C-Tier): Frog Eyes — Doubles his attack power and skips the enemy's upcoming turn. (Cooldown: 2 Turns)
  • ✝️ Miss Rita (C-Tier): Component card required for summoning the Nightmare Duo Fusion entity. (Cooldown: 3 Turns)
  • 🗣️ Mr. Maher (D-Tier): Talks Too Much — Forces the enemy to forfeit their next turn through sheer relentless talking. (Cooldown: 2 Turns)
  • ⚡ Miss Nada (D-Tier): Double Strike — Instantly doubles her attack output for a heavy strike. (Cooldown: 4 Turns)

⚡ Support Traps

  • 💖 Heal: Restores +600 HP to player or chosen fighter.
  • 💨 Evade: Dodges incoming opponent strike.
  • ⚡ Extra Credit: Grants +200 ATK bonus to active cards.
  • 🛑 Detention Slip: Forces enemy turn skip.
  • 🔔 School Bell: Instantly resets ability cooldown timers.
